Food

This is probably my favorite section to build for Emily.

It might take months, maybe even a year, because the idea is much bigger than just showing a list of restaurants.

Emily likes to eat, so I want to build something that helps her find food based on how she is feeling.

The idea is simple. It will be like a personal food finder made for Emily.

She can choose her mood or how she feels at that moment. Maybe she is happy, sad, excited, angry, cold, warm, sick, tired, stressed, or whatever she happens to be feeling.

And according to that mood, the site will suggest food that might feel right for her at that moment.

It will also look for places close to her, show her different options, and eventually make it easy for her to choose something and order it.

So instead of asking,

“What should I eat?”

she can simply tell the site,

“This is how I feel.”

And the site will try to find the food that matches that feeling/health condition.

That is the idea.

A personal food finder for Emily.
